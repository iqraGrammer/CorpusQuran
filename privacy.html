Privacy Policy

Corpus Quran - Word-by-Word Arabic Analysis

Last Updated: January 2024

Overview

Corpus Quran ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Summary: Corpus Quran does not collect any personal data. All your bookmarks and settings are stored locally on your device and never leave it.

Information We Do NOT Collect

Corpus Quran is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Browsing history
  • Contact information
  • Photos, videos, or media files
  • Health or fitness data
  • Financial information

Information Stored Locally

The app stores the following data only on your device:

  • Bookmarks: Verses, roots, and verbs you save for quick access
  • Settings Preferences: Font sizes, theme choices, and display options
  • Selected Translations: Your preferred translation selections

This data is stored using iOS's built-in UserDefaults system and never leaves your device.

Data Collection by Third Parties

Quran.com API

The app makes network requests to the Quran.com API (api.quran.com) to fetch:

  • Verse translations
  • Transliteration data

Additionally, the app may utilize morphological and linguistic data sourced from the Quranic Arabic Corpus (corpus.quran.com), which provides detailed grammatical and morphology analysis of Quranic Arabic.

These requests are made over secure HTTPS connections. Please refer to Quran.com's Privacy Policy for information about their data practices.

Apple

If you download the app from the App Store, Apple may collect certain information as described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Google Play Store

If you download the app through the Google Play Store, Google may collect certain information in accordance with Google Play’s Privacy Policy and Google’s general data practices.

Data Storage and Security

  • All user preferences are stored locally on your device
  • No data is transmitted to our servers (we don't operate any servers)
  • No user accounts or authentication required
  • No cloud sync or backup of app data

Children's Privacy

Corpus Quran does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. The app is suitable for users of all ages and contains no objectionable content.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media platforms
  • Crash reporting services

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal information to access, modify, or delete. Your locally stored preferences can be cleared by uninstalling the app.
